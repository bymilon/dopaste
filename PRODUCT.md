# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who need to write Markdown, save it quickly, and share a permanent read-only link without creating an account. They may be on desktop during focused work or on mobile while sharing a note, snippet, or short document.

## Product Purpose

Dopaste is a minimal Markdown pastebin. Success means a user can create a paste in seconds, get a stable URL, and have that shared paste load quickly through the Cloudflare edge stack.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, fast, direct. The interface should feel like a trustworthy tool: low-friction, keyboard-friendly, and free of social or publishing-platform noise.

## Anti-references

Avoid account-driven publishing suites, social feeds, ornate editor chrome, marketing-heavy dashboards, and decorative UI that competes with the writing task.

## Design Principles

- Keep paste creation as the obvious first action.
- Favor speed and clarity over feature count.
- Make public paste behavior explicit.
- Keep desktop efficient and mobile usable.
- Preserve a calm dark writing surface with accessible contrast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for contrast, keyboard operation, semantic forms, visible focus, and clear error messaging. Respect reduced-motion preferences if motion is added later.
